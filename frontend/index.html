Limitations

This system reflects historical survey data through 2015 and does not receive live updates. Estimates describe the most recent modeled status, not a forecast into an unobserved future year. Intervals are statistically calibrated against historical forecast error, not raw model confidence. The Graph Neural ODE architecture, evaluated during this project, is not used to serve predictions, as it was consistently the least reliable model tested.
Built on XGBoost, LightGBM, and a Transformer, trained on 116 years of survey data. Not for clinical or operational use.
